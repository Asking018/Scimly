Frequently Asked Questions

Is Scimly Guard a subscription?

No. During the launch period, Scimly Guard is available as a one-time purchase. Your license includes lifetime updates for the edition you purchase.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Scimly Guard is designed with a local-first approach. Directory data and CSV files are processed on your Windows PC and are not uploaded to Scimly servers.

What happens after 31 August 2026?

The current launch offer ends on 31 August 2026.

Customers who purchase before this date permanently keep:

  • Lifetime license
  • Lifetime updates
  • Higher device activation limits

We plan to transition future customers to standard device limits and time-based licenses (2- or 5-year terms). Existing launch customers will retain their lifetime license and launch device limits.

Can I upgrade to another edition later?

Yes. If your requirements change, you can upgrade from Base, Google Workspace, or Microsoft Entra ID to the Full Suite by paying only the price difference between your current edition and the Full Suite price. (Or update this answer if you plan a different upgrade policy.)

Do I need Microsoft Entra ID or Google Workspace credentials?

Only if you're using the Google Workspace Edition, Microsoft Entra ID Edition, or Full Suite for live directory analysis.

The Base Edition works with exported CSV files and doesn't require a live directory connection.

What's the difference between the four editions?
Edition Best For

Can I activate my license on multiple computers?

Yes. The number of Windows devices depends on the edition you purchase. Customers who buy during the launch period permanently keep the higher activation limits shown on this page.

Do I need an internet connection?

An internet connection is required for license activation, automatic updates, and live directory connections.

The Base Edition can continue analyzing previously exported CSV files offline after activation.

Currently, Scimly Guard is available for Windows.

Why is Scimly Guard much more affordable than many enterprise identity governance platforms?

Scimly Guard is designed for organizations that need practical access reviews, SCIM readiness analysis, and executive reporting without the complexity of a full enterprise identity governance platform. It focuses on solving common access review workflows with a lightweight Windows desktop application rather than replacing large enterprise IAM solutions.
